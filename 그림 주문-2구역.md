# 그림 주문 · 2구역 옛 왕도 수로

명단은 `잊혀진 기사 키우기 세계관/몬스터 도감.md`의 2구역(제안)이다. 명단을 확정한 뒤에 뽑는다.

## 뽑는 법
1. 제미나이 모델이 **Pro**인지 본다.
2. **대화 하나**에서 네 번에 나눠 뽑는다. 첫 주문에 `art/monsters/원본/1-07-늑대.png`를 함께 올린다.
3. 한 번에 세 장이 다 안 나오면 "나머지도 같은 규칙으로, 한 장씩 따로 그려 줘"라고 보낸다.
4. 나온 그림을 대화에 올린다. 배경 지우기와 들판에 놓아 보기는 코드로 한다.

## 첫 번째 (2-1 ~ 2-3)

```
Create THREE separate images, one creature per image. Do not combine them into one sheet. Generate all three, one after another.

STYLE (identical for all, and matching the attached reference image): realistic fantasy bestiary illustration, bold black ink outlines, dense cross-hatching for form and shadow, rich muted watercolour-like colours. Highly detailed dry textures such as fur, feathers, stone and metal. Same line weight and level of detail as the reference.

MOOD (very important): these are game monsters, not horror creatures. Menacing and fierce, but NOT disgusting. No slime, no dripping fluids, no drool, no exposed flesh, no rows of extra teeth, no insects, no gore, no blood. The threat comes from the pose, the glaring eyes and the expression. Never cute either: no big round eyes, no smiling.

RULES (identical for all): one single creature only, whole body visible, filling the frame without touching the edges. The creature faces LEFT in a three-quarter side view. Clearly visible eyes glaring toward the viewer's left. Flat pure white background (#FFFFFF), no ground, no water, no shadow, no mist, no scenery, no text, no border. Square image.

SETTING for this series: the stone canals of an old royal city at night.

IMAGE 1: CANAL RAT
A large, lean sewer rat the size of a dog, the weakest monster of the canals. Scruffy fur, a notched ear, a long bare tail curled behind it. It has turned to fight: back arched, front teeth bared, one old worn coin clenched in its mouth. Narrow angry eyes. Main colour: grey-brown fur.

IMAGE 2: HERON
A tall grey heron turned into a monster: a very long, sharp, spear-like beak aimed forward, neck drawn back in an S ready to strike, wings half spread, long legs planted. Cold pale eyes, a ragged black crest. Main colour: white and blue-grey feathers.

IMAGE 3: RIVER OTTER
A big, muscular river otter, fierce rather than playful. Low lunging pose, wet-looking but tidy fur (no slime), whiskers flared, mouth open in a snarl showing short sharp teeth, small glaring eyes. Main colour: dark chocolate brown with a paler throat.
```

## 두 번째 (2-4 ~ 2-6)

```
Same technique and exactly the same rules as before, including the flat pure white background with no water, no shadow and no mist. Create THREE separate images, one creature per image.

IMAGE 1: STONE CRAB
A large crab whose shell looks like a slab of old brick wall: flat, heavy and cracked, with a few bricks and a patch of moss on its back. Two thick claws raised and open. Small stalk eyes glaring. Hard and dry like stone and armour, not slimy, not spidery, with sturdy legs. Main colour: dull brick red.

IMAGE 2: SNAPPING TURTLE
A huge snapping turtle. A heavy ridged shell covered in moss, a thick neck stretched forward, a hooked beak open and ready to bite, small furious eyes, clawed feet braced. Slow, heavy and dangerous. Main colour: mossy green shell with a dark olive body.

IMAGE 3: STONE GARGOYLE
A gargoyle statue from a bridge railing, come to life. Carved grey stone body with chips and cracks, bat-like stone wings spread, crouched and about to pounce, clawed hands forward, a snarling horned face with two small glowing eyes. It is clearly made of stone. Main colour: cold stone grey.
```

## 세 번째 (2-7 ~ 2-9)

```
Same technique and exactly the same rules as before, including the flat pure white background with no water, no shadow and no mist. Create THREE separate images, one creature per image.

IMAGE 1: EMPTY ARMOUR
A suit of old city-guard plate armour walking with nobody inside. A dented helmet with a dark empty visor in which two small points of pale light glow, a tattered tabard, a long halberd held in both gauntlets. The armour is covered in blue-green verdigris and rust. Stiff, heavy, advancing stance. Main colour: verdigris blue-green over dark iron.

IMAGE 2: KOBOLD
A small dog-faced canal bandit standing upright: a scruffy mongrel face with a long snout, torn pointed ears and bared teeth, wearing a ragged red hood and patched leather. It holds a small lit lantern in one hand and a short hooked blade in the other. Sly, mean eyes. About the size of a goblin. Main colour: dirty yellow-tan fur with a red hood.

IMAGE 3: LIZARD SOLDIER
A tall, lean lizardman soldier, bigger and more dangerous than the kobold. Dry scaly skin, a long snout, a crest of spines down the neck, a thick tail. He wears pieces of rusted armour and thrusts a long spear with a chipped blade. Cold yellow slit eyes. Main colour: olive-green scales with dull bronze armour.
```

## 네 번째 (2-10 보스)

```
Same technique and exactly the same rules as before, including the flat pure white background with no water, no shadow and no mist.

Draw the BOSS of this series: the SLUICE-GATE CROCODILE. He must look far more powerful and dangerous than every other creature in the series.

- Body: a colossal ancient crocodile, three times the bulk of the lizard soldier. A massive armoured back with thick ridged scales, a huge heavy tail, short powerful legs with great claws.
- Pose: reared up at the front, jaws opening, about to lunge. Towering and threatening.
- Chains: heavy rusted iron chains from a broken sluice gate are wrapped around his neck and shoulders, with a broken iron ring and a piece of splintered gate timber still hanging from them.
- Head: a broad scarred snout, one old broken tooth, fierce pale-gold eyes fixed on the viewer's left. Large teeth, but a normal crocodile's number of teeth. No drool, no gore.
- Main colour: very dark blue-green scales with a paler ochre belly and orange-brown rust on the chains.

He faces LEFT in a three-quarter side view. Only the creature, nothing on the ground. The whole figure fills the frame without touching the edges. Genuinely terrifying, never comical.
```
