# 그림 주문 · 1구역 배경

기준은 `가이드라인.md`의 "맵 배경"이다. 가로 화면(16:9), 먼 배경은 어둡고 사실적으로, 발판은 밝은 2D로.

## 뽑는 법
1. 제미나이 **Pro**, 새 대화. 그림은 올리지 않는다.
2. 먼 배경과 발판은 따로 뽑는다(대화도 따로).
3. 먼 배경은 가로로 긴 그림(16:9)으로 나와야 한다. 정사각형으로 나오면 "16:9 가로로 다시 그려 줘"라고 보낸다.
4. 나온 그림을 대화에 올린다. 발판을 자르고 배경을 이어 붙이는 일은 코드로 한다.

## 1-1 · 숲 들머리 (냄비 쓴 다람쥐) · 먼 배경

```
A wide background painting for a 2D side-scrolling fantasy game. Aspect ratio 16:9, landscape.

Scene: the edge of an old forest at early dawn, seen from the side at eye level. Huge old oak and beech trees with thick trunks stand in rows going far back into the distance. Soft pale-gold morning light comes through the leaves in a few shafts. Between the trees, far away, a dirt road leads out toward low hills and one small village with warm windows. Dew on ferns and moss. Calm, quiet, a little lonely.

Style: detailed, painterly, semi-realistic fantasy landscape painting. Deep, dark, muted colours: dark greens, blue-greens and brown, with only the light shafts bright. Low contrast overall and slightly soft focus, because this is the far layer behind the game characters.

Important layout rules:
- The lower third of the picture is darker and plainer (shadowed undergrowth), with no strong shapes, because game platforms will be drawn on top of it.
- The left edge and the right edge look alike (similar trees and light), so the picture can be repeated side by side.
- No characters, no animals, no buildings close up, no path in the foreground, no text, no frame, no fog covering the scene.
```

## 1-1 · 숲 들머리 · 발판

```
Game art assets for a 2D side-scrolling fantasy game: platform pieces. Square image, PURE WHITE background (#FFFFFF), no shadows on the background, no text.

Draw FOUR separate pieces, stacked from top to bottom with clear white space between them, never touching:
1. A long ground strip, as wide as the image: flat grassy top with small flowers and clover, and below it dark forest soil with roots and a few stones. The top edge is a flat straight line. The left end and right end match so it can repeat.
2. A medium floating platform, about half the image width: a thick slab of earth and roots with the same flat grassy top, rounded underneath, with a few hanging roots.
3. A small floating platform, about a quarter of the image width, same style.
4. A fallen mossy log lying on its side, about a third of the image width, flat enough on top to stand on.

Style: bright, clean 2D cartoon game art with dark outlines and simple cel shading, clear readable shapes. Fresh light greens and warm browns, much brighter and simpler than a realistic painting. Seen straight from the side. Original design, not based on any existing game.
```
