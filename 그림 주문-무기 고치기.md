# 그림 주문 · 무기 한 자루씩 고치기

네 자루가 든 그림을 통째로 다시 뽑지 않고, 마음에 안 드는 한 자루만 고칠 때 쓴다.

## 고치는 법
1. 고칠 검의 그림은 `art/무기/고치기/<이름>.png`에 있다(흰 배경에 한 자루). 없으면 `art/무기/<이름>.png`를 흰 바탕에 올려 만든다.
2. 제미나이 **Pro**, **새 대화**에서 그 그림 한 장을 올리고 아래 문구를 붙여 넣는다. 검 하나에 대화 하나.
3. 두 번 고쳐도 이상하면 그 대화는 버리고, 같은 그림으로 새 대화에서 다시 한다. 고친 그림을 또 고치면 그림이 망가진다.
4. 나온 그림을 대화에 올린다. 자르고 바꿔 끼우는 일은 코드로 한다.

## 1 · 사냥꾼의 칼 (손잡이가 나뭇가지처럼 보임)

```
Edit this image of a sword. Keep everything exactly the same: the drawing style, outlines, shading, size, position and angle, the pure white background, and every part I do not mention.

Change ONLY the grip. Right now it looks like a rough brown wooden branch. Redraw it as a smooth handle made of deer antler: pale ivory colour with soft tan shading, a gentle curve, a few fine carved rings, and a small steel cap at the end. It must clearly read as polished antler, not wood or bark.

Keep the dark blade, the guard and the green cord exactly as they are. Still one single sword, lying horizontal with the tip on the left. Pure white background, no shadow, no text.
```

## 2 · 가시덩굴 검 (손잡이가 잘린 나무토막처럼 보임)

```
Edit this image of a sword. Keep everything exactly the same: the drawing style, outlines, shading, size, position and angle, the pure white background, and every part I do not mention.

Change ONLY the handle, which is everything to the right of the red rose. Right now it looks like a cut wooden branch. Replace it with a proper sword hilt of the same length: a grip wrapped in dark green leather with one thin gold vine spiralling around it, and a pommel shaped like a golden rosebud holding a small emerald. No wood, no bark, no cut log end.

Keep the green blade, the thorny vines, the leaves and the red rose exactly as they are. Still one single sword, lying horizontal with the tip on the left. Pure white background, no shadow, no text.
```

## 3 · 등불 검 (영웅 등급치고 칙칙함)

```
Edit this image of a sword. Keep the same drawing style, outlines, size, position and angle, the same overall shape of the sword, and the pure white background.

This sword looks too dull and grey for a high-grade item. Make it look precious:
- The blade becomes polished dark steel with a thin gold line along both edges.
- The long window in the blade glows rich warm orange-gold along its whole length, with no white part.
- The lantern-shaped guard becomes polished gold with glowing amber glass panels.
- The grip becomes deep red leather with two gold bands.
- The pommel becomes a gold cap holding a small amber gem.
The glow stays inside the outline of the sword.

Still one single sword, lying horizontal with the tip on the left. Pure white background, no shadow, no glow on the background, no text.
```

## 4 · 기사의 긴 검 (기사단장의 검과 색이 같아서 헷갈림)

```
Edit this image of a sword. Keep everything exactly the same: the drawing style, outlines, shading, shapes, size, position and angle, the pure white background, and every part I do not mention.

Change ONLY colours on the hilt: every blue part (the blue enamel on the crossguard, the blue grip, the blue gem in the pommel) becomes deep emerald green. The small white parts on the crossguard become gold. Nothing blue remains anywhere.

Keep the blade with its gold line exactly as it is. Still one single sword, lying horizontal with the tip on the left. Pure white background, no shadow, no text.
```

## 5 · 기사단장의 검 (기사의 긴 검과 비슷하고, 영웅치고 수수함)

```
Edit this image of a sword. Keep the same drawing style, outlines, size, position and angle, and the pure white background.

Make this sword grander: it is the sword of the commander of an order of knights.
- Keep the white blade with the gold line down its centre, and add a short band of fine gold engraving on the blade next to the guard.
- Replace the guard with a wider one shaped like two spread golden wings, with one large round sapphire in the centre.
- Keep the royal blue grip and add three thin gold bands to it.
- Replace the pommel with a small golden lion's head.
- Add a short royal blue tassel at the pommel. It must be short: it does not hang lower than the guard.

Still one single sword, lying horizontal with the tip on the left. Pure white background, no shadow, no text.
```

## 6 · 기억의 검 (게임에서 가장 좋은 검인데 손잡이가 녹슬고 낡아 보임)

```
Edit this image of a sword. Keep everything exactly the same: the drawing style, outlines, size, position and angle, the pure white background, and above all the crystal blade (icy blue at the tip, white in the middle, warm orange near the handle). Do not change the blade at all.

Change ONLY the hilt, which now looks rusty and worn out:
- The lantern-shaped guard becomes polished dark silver with gold trim, with a bright warm orange light inside it.
- The grip becomes neatly wrapped dark navy cord. Keep one small cream-coloured cloth patch stitched onto the grip (this patch is important).
- Replace the rusty pommel with a round gem set in gold: one half of the gem is icy blue and the other half is warm orange.
No rust, no dirt, no dull grey metal.

Still one single sword, lying horizontal with the tip on the left. Pure white background, no shadow, no text.
```
